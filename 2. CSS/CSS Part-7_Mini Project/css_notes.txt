# Basic Design Principles 
        for better looking websites (better UI Design--> attract users,more use)

        --color, --typography

        --Color Theory
            color wheel (primary colors-red,green,blue)

            meaning of colors, their application   (like as yellow color is bright, noticeable, it is used extensively for advertisements & like black, gray & white color provides luxury/premium look to the website)

            colors, as per their interpretation gives deeper meaning somewhat to the users , helping in attraction & trust. 
            for eg- In razorpay, use of blue color for stability & reality. In netflix, use of red for excitement


            subtle color combination - Monochromatic (using a light color, and its lighter shades for a light look)   
            impactful color combination - Complementary (using dark colors, and such similar for a impactful look) 
            high contrast combination - Triadic 

            canva color wheel for to analyse combinations and color look on websites
